# Privacy Policy — Nurture

**Last updated: October 2026**

## The short version
Nurture does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

## Data storage
- All feeding logs, diaper logs, burp logs, and appointment entries are stored **only** in a local database on your device.
- Nurture has **no user accounts, no sign-up, and no servers**. There is nowhere for your data to be sent.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.

## Data collection
- Nurture collects **no personal information** and **no usage analytics**.
- Nurture contains **no advertising SDKs** and **no third-party tracking SDKs**.
- The app does not access your contacts, location, photos, or any other device data beyond what is required for its core features (local notifications for appointment reminders, file sharing when you explicitly export a PDF).

## Notifications
Appointment reminders are scheduled entirely on your device using the operating system's local notification service. No reminder data ever leaves your phone.

## PDF export
When you export a report, the PDF is generated on your device and shared only through the share option **you** choose (email, messaging, printing, etc.). Nurture never uploads or sees the exported file.

## Children's privacy
Nurture is a tool for parents and caregivers to log newborn care. Because all data remains on the parent's device and nothing is collected or transmitted, no children's personal information is ever gathered by the developer.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published with the app update. The core promise — your data stays on your device — will not change.
