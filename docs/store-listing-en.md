## Full description
**Nurture is the simple, free newborn tracker built for 3 AM.**

Log a bottle in under 5 seconds with one thumb. Track breastfeeding, wet and dirty diapers, and burps — then hand your pediatrician a clean PDF report of any date range.

**Why parents love Nurture:**
- 🍼 One-tap bottle logging with mL or oz — your choice
- 🤱 Built-in nursing timer with left/right tracking
- 💧 One-tap diaper logging: wet, dirty, or both
- 🫧 Burp tracking
- 📅 Pediatrician visit calendar with automatic reminders
- 📄 PDF reports for any date range — ready for the doctor
- 🌙 Dark mode and huge buttons, designed for half-asleep logging
- 🇪🇸 Full English & Spanish support

**Private by design:**
Nurture stores everything on your phone. No account, no signup, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your baby's data never leaves your device.

Free forever. No subscriptions, no paywalls.
